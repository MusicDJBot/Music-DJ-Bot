How it works

Powered by multi-source music catalog intelligence and built for people who actually listen to music in Discord.

Text chat or 256kbps voice

Don't force everyone into a voice room just to hear a song. Listen inline in text chat at your own pace, or run /vplay to stream 256kbps Opus audio live in voice.

Zero chat clutter

By default, new songs update a single active Song Card in place and status commands reply privately. Prefer a scrolling history of every song played? Toggle /history state: On anytime.

Liked Songs library (❤️ & /likes)

Click ❤️ on any Song Card to save it without interrupting playback. Stream your personal favorites or a combined Server Liked Songs mix anytime with /likes.

One-click DJ deck

Every Song Card includes buttons to skip (⏭️), save to Liked Songs (❤️), spin a random genre (🎲), or toggle hands-free Autoplay (📻)—plus similarity radio via /similar.

Studio tracks only (no AI spam)

Automatically blocks short-form clips (<65s), livestreams, gaming videos, reaction clips, AI-generated music spam, and 3-hour mixes. Only real studio songs (65s–15m) get through.

Playlists, queues & server volume

Pre-resolves upcoming tracks for instant skips. Load playlists with /playlist, inspect upcoming tracks with /queue, and save custom server volume (0%–200%) with /volume.

Commands

Type / in Discord to use autocomplete, or click an example to copy.

/play [query]
Play top hits (if blank), a genre, Song by Artist, or a supported music link
/playlist <url> [shuffle] [loop]
Load a supported music playlist URL with optional shuffle and loop
/artist <name>
Play top studio tracks from an artist's catalog
/similar <song> [artist]
Start a radio station of tracks similar to a song
/likes [action] [song]
Play or manage your personal & server Liked Songs playlists with shuffle and loop
/queue
View the channel's Now Playing track and upcoming session queue
/history [state]
Toggle Single Card Mode (Off, default) or scrolling Song Card history (On)
/vplay [query] PRO
Join your voice channel and stream 256kbps Opus audio immediately
/voice <action> [volume] PRO
Join, pause, resume, skip, view queue, adjust volume, or leave voice
/volume [level] PRO
View or set server voice playback volume from 0% to 200% (default 85%)
/autoplay [state] [genre] PRO
Automatically advance to the next song when the current track ends
/clear & /help
Reset the channel's recently played history or view the private command guide

FAQ

Is Server Pro really a one-time payment?

Yes. A single $4 (crypto) or $5 (card/Apple Pay) payment unlocks dedicated Voice Channel Streaming, custom server volume, Hands-Free Autoplay, and expanded Liked Songs storage (100/user & 1,000/server) for your Discord server permanently. There are no recurring fees.

How do Liked Songs (❤️ & /likes) work?

Click ❤️ under any Song Card to save it to your personal and server Liked Songs library without interrupting playback (the bot replies privately with a 💔 Remove button in case you change your mind). Use /likes anytime to stream your personal favorites, play a combined mix of every song liked in your server, or add/remove tracks.

How does Single Card Mode keep chat clean?

By default (/history state: Off), every new song updates the active Song Card in place rather than posting a new message, and status commands reply privately to you. If you prefer a scrolling feed where every song posts a new card, run /history state: On.

What queries and links are supported?

Paste any song name, artist, or supported music link into /play or /vplay. The bot verifies that it is a genuine studio track between 65 seconds and 15 minutes and filters out AI music spam and non-music clips. For full playlists, pass a supported playlist URL to /playlist.

How do I restrict the bot to specific channels?

You can control which channels the bot can access directly inside Discord under Server Settings → Integrations → Music DJ Bot.
